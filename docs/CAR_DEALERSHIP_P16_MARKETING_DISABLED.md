# P16 marketing stays disabled

P16 adds a central policy that keeps public newsletter signup and promotional campaign delivery disabled. The public footer no longer collects email addresses, and the signup endpoint rejects requests before rate limiting or database access. Newsletter administration remains limited to consent records; staff can unsubscribe or suppress a record but cannot re-enable it on a customer’s behalf.

No campaign sender, queue, or marketing message is introduced. P15 customer channel preferences remain separate and default to off. No live messaging was enabled.
